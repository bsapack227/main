Cub Scout Organization Explained

Cub Scouts is for girls and boys from kindergarten through fifth grade. A Cub Scout Pack is made up of Dens, organized by grade level. Each Den works separately on a number of age-appropriate Adventures, and scouts earn patches and belt loops for completing these Adventures. All of the Dens, collectively, make up the Pack.

The Pack is led by a Cubmaster and Committee Chairperson, and each Den is led by two Den Leaders, all of whom are parent volunteers.

Our Pack includes the following Dens:

  • Kindergarten – LION
  • First Grade – TIGER
  • Second Grade – WOLF
  • Third Grade – BEAR
  • Fourth Grade – WEBELOS
  • Fifth Grade - ARROW OF LIGHT


Lion Den The Lion Den (Den 5) is for Cub Scouts who are in kindergarten. Each Lion scout must be accompanied by a Lion Adult Partner (usually a parent) during all activities.


Tiger Den The Tiger Den (Den 4) is for Cub Scouts who are in first grade. Each Tiger scout must be accompanied by a Tiger Adult Partner (usually a parent) during all activities.


Wolf Den The Wolf Den (Den 3) is for Cub Scouts who are in second grade.


Bear Den The Bear Den (Den 2) is for Cub Scouts who are in third grade.


WEBELOS Den The Webelos Den (Den 1) is for Cub Scouts in fourth grade. "Webelos" stands for "WE'll BE LOyal Scouts!"


Arrow Of Light Den The Arrow of Light Den is for Cub Scouts in fifth grade. This den will be selecting a Patrol Name in Fall 2025.

Please contact Pack 227 if you're interested in learning more.
