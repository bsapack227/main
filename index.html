About Us

A Scout Pack
Built on Community

Pack 227 is a welcoming, family-driven Cub Scout pack serving the Agoura Hills and the Greater Conejo Valley from Thousand Oaks to Calabasas. Whether your child is joining their very first den or transferring from another pack, you'll find enthusiastic families ready to make every adventure count.

Cub Scouts is about more than badges - it's about building character, making friends, and learning skills that last a lifetime. Our pack brings together kids in kindergarten through fifth grade for hands-on fun, from campfires and pinewood derbies to STEM projects and community service.

New to Scouting? No problem. No prior experience is needed - for kids or parents. Our experienced den leaders and pack committee are here to guide every family from day one.

Why Pack 227

Everything a Great
Scout Pack Should Be

🏕️
Outdoor Adventures

Camping trips, nature hikes, and hands-on exploration that get kids off screens and into the world around them.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Family-Friendly Culture

We plan events that the whole family can enjoy - not just the scouts. Parents and siblings are always part of the fun.

🌟
No Experience Needed

First-time scouts and first-time scout families are our specialty. Every new member gets a warm welcome and plenty of support.

💛
Girls & Boys Welcome

Pack 227 is proud to have a great mix of boy and girl scouts across all dens, building friendships across the whole community.

📅
Twice-Monthly Meetings

We meet twice a month - enough to build lasting bonds and earn achievements without overloading busy family schedules.

🌍
Community Service

We participate in local community events such as the 4th of July parade, Reyes Adobe Parade, City Council flag ceremonies, and special projects each year.
